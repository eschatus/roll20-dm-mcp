# Moved — the QLoRA training rig now lives in `dm-whisper`

Mothballed 2026-08-18. The rig is at **`dm-whisper/training/`**:
`train_qlora.py`, `smoke_test.py`, `Modelfile.template`, `requirements.txt`, `RUNBOOK.md`.

It was here only for historical reasons — the gem was a `voice-hud/` subdirectory of this
repo until 2026-08-11. It trains the *gem's* narration→tool-call specialist against a corpus
generated by the gem's own `scripts/gen-traces.ts`, and evaluated by the gem's own
`scripts/eval-golden.ts`. Nothing in this MCP server depends on it, and keeping a second copy
here only invites the two drifting apart.

The pre-registration and measurement records stay here, because they are this repo's history:

- `SFT_RUN_STATE.md` — the measured hardware verdict (local training is out) and open items
- `SFT_TRACE_PLAN.md` — the complexity-axis design that became the 14 axes
- `SPECIALIST_MODEL_DECISION.md` — the pre-registered "should we train at all" analysis
- `docs/table-mechanics-golden-pairs.md` — the conventions the whole corpus encodes

`dm-whisper/training/RUNBOOK.md` cites them with a `roll20-dm-mcp/` prefix.
